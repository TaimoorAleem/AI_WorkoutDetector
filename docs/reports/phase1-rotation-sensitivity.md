The held-out participant's acc and gyro columns are rotated by the same R before feature engineering. Models are trained on unrotated data, except the augmented variants. Augmentation method: the training set is the original (unrotated) training sets PLUS K rotated copies of them (not K copies only); each copy of each set gets its own random R (uniform random axis, angle uniform in [0, max]), applied to acc and gyro before feature engineering; same seed for every augmented variant. R2: K=4, max 15 deg; R2w: K=4, max 30 deg; R2w8: K=8, max 30 deg. Metrics are means over the 5 LOPO folds. 'Worst axis' = the signed axis (+-x, +-y, +-z) with the lowest set accuracy at that theta; 'rand' = mean over 10 seeded random axes. 'Kabsch' is the equivalent gravity residual 2 sin(theta/2) in g. 'conf' is the pooled bench->OHP + OHP->bench window rate over bench and OHP windows.
