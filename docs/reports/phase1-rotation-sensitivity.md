The held-out participant's acc and gyro columns are rotated by the same R before feature engineering. Models are trained on unrotated data (R2: the original training sets plus 4 copies under random rotations, angle U[0, 15] deg, random axis, one R per set copy). Metrics are means over the 5 LOPO folds. 'Worst axis' = the signed axis (+-x, +-y, +-z) with the lowest set accuracy at that theta; 'rand' = mean over 10 seeded random axes. 'Kabsch' is the equivalent gravity residual 2 sin(theta/2) in g. 'conf' is the pooled bench->OHP + OHP->bench window rate over bench and OHP windows.

## theta_tol (section 7.3 definition)

Largest theta where, over the worst axis, set accuracy drops <= 2 pp and bench and OHP recall each drop <= 5 pp vs theta = 0. 'signed' considers the six signed axes; 'all' also includes the random axes.
