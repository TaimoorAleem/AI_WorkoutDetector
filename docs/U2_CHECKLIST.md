# U2 gym session checklist (left wrist)

> Two sessions, **at least 2 days apart**. Session 1 is used to fit the watch's axes. Session 2 is the held-out test, so **please do it just as you'd train normally**.
> Each session: 5 exercises × (2 heavy + 2 medium) = **20 sets**, plus rest between them and 5 short "other" periods. Plan on about 60–75 min.

## Before you go
- [ ] Watch and phone **charged** (the watch screen stays on while recording).
- [ ] Phone: Proprio set up (participant **P01**, consent on). Watch on your **left wrist**, snug, in its usual spot.
- [ ] Pick weights per exercise:
  - **heavy** = a weight you can do for **5 reps** with 1–2 in reserve;
  - **medium** = a weight for **10 reps** with 1–2 in reserve.
  - Write them down; you'll enter the kg in the app.

## How to record each set
1. On the phone: pick the **exercise**, **load (kg)** and **category** (heavy/medium).
2. Get into position (bar racked, hands on the bar), then tap **Start set**.
3. Do the reps. **Count them out loud.** Don't touch the watch.
4. Rack the bar, tap **Stop**, and enter **the reps you actually did**. If it wasn't 5 or 10, enter the real number; that's fine.
5. Rest 60–120 s. **Rest is recorded automatically** between sets; you don't need to tap anything.

If you make a mistake, keep going. Use **Discard** for accidental segments, or fix it later in **Review**, or write a note.

## Session order
Do the exercises in blocks (all four sets of one exercise, then move on):

| # | Session 1 | Session 2 (different order on purpose) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Squat: heavy, heavy, medium, medium | Deadlift |
| 2 | Bench: heavy, heavy, medium, medium | OHP |
| 3 | Row (barbell): heavy, heavy, medium, medium | Squat |
| 4 | OHP: heavy, heavy, medium, medium | Row |
| 5 | Deadlift: heavy, heavy, medium, medium | Bench |

## "Other" periods (5 per session, about 30 s each)
Tap **Start other** on the phone, do it, then tap **Stop other**. Spread them through the session:
- [ ] walking to the next station
- [ ] loading or unloading plates
- [ ] drinking water or using your phone
- [ ] adjusting the bench or rack
- [ ] stretching or warm-up arm swings

These teach the system what *not* to count as a lift. The training data has none.

## Tips
- **Start set just before the first rep, and stop right after the last.** A few seconds either way is fine.
- **Use the same movement standard as always.** Barbell, not dumbbells, for these five exercises. If you can only do a variant (e.g. dumbbell bench), write it in the note.
- **Optional but valuable: video.** Prop up a second device to film sets, so rep counts can be verified later.

## After the session
- [ ] Phone: **End session**, then check the **Recordings** tab shows it, with 20 sets.
- [ ] Open **Review**, skim the labels, and fix any wrong exercise or reps.
- [ ] Put the watch on the charger, turn on **Wireless debugging** on both devices, and tell me.
